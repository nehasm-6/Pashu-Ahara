##Project Name:
Android App Development using GenAI - Pashu-Aahar (Agriculture)
This is the description: Pashu-Aahara is a GenAI-based cattle nutrition calculator application designed to help dairy farmers prepare balanced and cost-effective feed plans for their cattle. The app analyzes cattle details such as age, weight, milk production, and health condition to recommend suitable nutrition using locally available feed ingredients. It also supports cattle profile management, feed tracking, bilingual access, and offline usage, making it useful and accessible for rural farmers.
##Features: 
Feature1: Cattle Profile Management – Store details like age, weight, breed, milk production, and health status of cattle.
Feature2: AI-Based Feed Recommendation – Generates balanced and nutritious feed plans automatically.
Feature3: Cost-Effective Feed Planning – Suggests affordable feed combinations using locally available ingredients.
Feature4: Nutrition Calculation – Calculates required nutrients for cattle growth and milk production.
Feature5: Feed Ingredient Database – Contains nutritional values of common cattle feed ingredients.
Feature6: Milk Production Support – Helps improve milk yield through optimized nutrition plans.
Feature7: Health Monitoring Support – Provides feed adjustments based on cattle health conditions.
Feature8: Bilingual Support – Available in regional language and English for easy understanding.
Feature9: Offline Access – Works without internet connection for rural farmers.
Feature10: User-Friendly Interface – Simple and easy-to-use design for all users.
